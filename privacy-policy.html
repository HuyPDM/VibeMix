Music Box: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Music Box is a browser extension for making music by layering loops. It is built to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Music Box does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your songs and preferences (such as filter choices and settings) are saved locally in your browser using the chrome.storage.local API, so your work is restored when you reopen the extension. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by removing the extension or using "Delete" in My songs.

Network access

Music Box makes no network requests. All sounds are generated in your browser with the Web Audio API.

Permissions

storage: used only to save your songs and preferences locally, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
